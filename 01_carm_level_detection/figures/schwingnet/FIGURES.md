Figure 1 is 7.00 x 7.85 in at print width, nothing scaled: the rows are (a) 2.02, (b) 0.95, (c) 2.60, (d) 2.10 in tall, each drawn at 6.76 in by its own tool, with the letters (14 pt Arial Bold) in a 0.24 in gutter. Every text on the page is Arial (Arial-BoldMT, ArialMT), the smallest 12 pt over 115 spans: labels (numerals, cues, stage and block names, level names) bold, descriptive lines regular (`tools/fig1_style.audit` refuses a panel with any text under 12 pt, off its canvas or on another text; `figure_set.check_text` re-reads the composed PDF). Colour carries two meanings only: the light-blue fill is a learned stage ((b) and (c)) and red is the pair of bodies being matched ((d)); everything else is black, grey or white. Production files: `figure1.tif`, 8400 x 9426 px at 1,200 dpi, RGB, LZW, 4.9 MB, rendered from the vector page (no raster in it, so nothing upsampled); `figure1.pdf`, the vector master with every label as live TrueType text. The manuscript prints it at `\linewidth` (16 cm), where the same text is 10.8 pt; the journal's 12 pt floor is met at the 7 in production width. The TIF is flattened (matplotlib writes no layers); the PDF is the editable form.

Panels of Figure 1 kept beside it as files: `fig1a_problem`, `fig2_pipeline`, `fig1c_arch`, `fig1d_algorithm` (PNG, PDF, caption sidecar each; each PDF is exactly 6.76 in wide). `fig5_confirm_fluoro.*` is the source of figure2. `tools/fig3_panorama.py` is imported by the (d) builder and, if run on its own, writes a `fig3_panorama.*` that is not in the set.

Table 1: `SchwingNet/tables/tab_corpus.tex` (sources traced in `tab_corpus.sources.md`, rows mirrored in `tab_corpus.md`, totals and VinDr counts also in `NUMBERS.md`). Standalone check: `slurm/tab_corpus_check.sh` on the grid (xelatex from the texlive/2020 module, at the manuscript geometry in the manuscript face and in a sans stress face), measured and rendered by `tools/tab_corpus_check.py` (every span >= 7 pt at a 7 in print width, one page, inside the text area), page images `tab_corpus.check.png` and `tab_corpus.check_sans.png`.
