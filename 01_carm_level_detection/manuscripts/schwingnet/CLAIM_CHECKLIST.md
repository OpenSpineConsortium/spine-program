## Standing rules applied

- The counting heuristic evaluated in docs/ANOMALY_RULE_EVALUATION.md does not hold and is not used, drawn or described anywhere in SchwingNet/.
- Only numbers with a case-level (cluster) interval, a stated operating point and a source file are quoted. Numbers from banks with a known split leak (allspine v2/v6/v7, v8 for VerSe, anchor_drr, vindrps) are not quoted.
- No commercial product and no regulatory pathway is named; docs/MODEL_CARD.md section 2.1 and docs/CARM_DEPLOYMENT_EVIDENCE.md name both and must not be lifted verbatim.
