Rule: a number enters `main.tex`, its sections, tables or figures only if it carries (i) a 95 % interval
clustered at the case, specimen, patient or fold, (ii) a stated operating point, and (iii) a file it is read
from. Counts (frames, cases, vertebrae) carry (iii). Nothing is quoted from a bank with a known split leak
(allspine v2, v6, v7; v8 for VerSe; anchor_drr; vindrps) or from `~/verse_drr` millimetres. Paths are relative
to the repository root; `#` marks a JSON path. Intervals are percentile unless marked BCa.
