# Availability, data sharing and ethics statements

Two versions of each statement: **FULL** for the full title page (not anonymized; names repositories and the
institution) and **MANUSCRIPT** for "Other information" in the anonymized `main.tex`, which the edit phase pastes in
place of the current paragraphs (this workflow does not edit `main.tex`). `build_packet_src.py` reads the blocks by
their `## BLOCK:` names; `{{GIT_HEAD}}` is filled at build time with the short revision of the tree that is built.
`[[CONFIRM: ...]]` marks a decision only the author can make; `[[PENDING Gx: ...]]` waits on a gate.

**The release of code and weights is not decided**, and the documents that bear on it disagree (evidence table,
last rows). The FULL data sharing statement therefore commits only to what is true now (private, available to
reviewers and editors on request) and carries a CONFIRM marker; the two ready wordings for publication are the blocks
`WEIGHTS_OPTION_DEPOSIT` and `WEIGHTS_OPTION_AGREEMENT`, each with its manuscript twin. Pick one, paste it over the
marker, and set `author_decisions.release_code_weights` in `front_matter.json`.

## BLOCK: DATA_SHARING_FULL

## BLOCK: WEIGHTS_OPTION_DEPOSIT

At publication the code, the fitted device parameters, the result files and the trained weights will be deposited publicly under a persistent identifier, with a SHA-256 hash for each weight file, for research use; because the training sources include CC BY-NC 4.0 and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 releases and a release under a data use agreement, the terms of the weights will be no less restrictive than non-commercial.

## BLOCK: WEIGHTS_OPTION_AGREEMENT

At publication the result files and the fitted device parameters will be deposited publicly under a persistent identifier; the code and the trained weights, each identified in that deposit by its revision or SHA-256 hash, will be available for non-commercial research under an agreement with the author.

## BLOCK: CODE_AVAILABILITY_MANUSCRIPT_DEPOSIT

Code, the fitted image-formation parameters (one file per device) and the trained weights are in a private repository available to the reviewers and editors on request; at publication they will be deposited publicly with a revision identifier and a SHA-256 hash per weight file, for research use under terms no less restrictive than the non-commercial licences of the training sources. The weights are released for retrospective analysis and for bench, phantom and cadaver evaluation, not for clinical use; nothing here has been evaluated prospectively.

## BLOCK: CODE_AVAILABILITY_MANUSCRIPT_AGREEMENT

## BLOCK: ETHICS_FULL

Secondary analysis of publicly available, de-identified imaging. The Wayne State University Institutional Review Board Administration Office issued a Notice of IRB Administrative Determination on 2 September 2026 (WSU IRB HPR Number 2026-269; "Open Spine Consortium: Secondary Analysis of Publicly Available, De-Identified Imaging Datasets and the Derived Annotations, Software, and Models Produced From Them") that the project is not human participant research under 45 CFR 46 or FDA regulations and does not require IRB review. The determination covers sources that are public and de-identified before release, downloadable without application or data use agreement, not restricted-use, carry no re-identification key and are not linked for re-identification. VinDr-SpineXR, released under the PhysioNet Restricted Health Data Use Agreement, is covered by a separate determination of the same office [[PENDING G0: VinDr-SpineXR determination number and date]]. No protected health information was accessed, so informed consent and HIPAA authorization do not apply. C-ArmLSpine and COBRA2026 are used under their releases (CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-NC 4.0), DeepFluoro and the Ljubljana set under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and CC BY-NC-ND. One C-ArmLSpine directory carrying a residual patient identifier was excluded before any processing and reported to the dataset's authors.
