AP = anteroposterior; CBCT = cone-beam CT; CDC = Centers for Disease Control and Prevention; RTK = reconstruction toolkit; TCIA = The Cancer Imaging Archive; level bank = the training set of the level encoder and head; R² = coefficient of determination.

- (a) Physics-styled re-renders of the three CT collections (7,045 over 3,029 subjects; 1,482 over 81 transitional subjects) repeat subjects already counted; bank subject counts are not additive.
- (b) Record 0533 was a truncated download and is in no trained bank; rebuilt and replaced in the release (802 records in the released corpus, supplement Table S3), it enters no reported number.
- (c) No patient identifier in the release: the scan, series or study is the finest recoverable unit; the TotalSegmentator count folds 24 renumbered pairs and three duplicate groups; VerSe series were keyed as subjects in the film bank, so no VerSe accuracy is quoted.
- (d) 3,598 by content: two case pairs share byte-identical films, which the built split merges (supplement Table S3).
- (e) The CDC index lists 17,075 films; two are served empty at the source, so 17,073 are on disk, and one annotated film (L00452) survives only inside the annotation archive.
- (f) Fold banks = the capped per-fold crop banks of the intraoperative cross-validation (about 31,400 crops per fold); the film bank behind the plain-film accuracies holds no VinDr or FluoroCap image. The released corpus takes all 10,466 VinDr images and drops FluoroCap (a republished figure is the same patient).
- (g) A panel keys to its source article or video, which may hold several patients or one patient republished.
- (h) The archive ships 299 spot exposures over 149 cases (149 AP, 149 lateral with one repeat, one oblique) and two 301-projection cone-beam sweeps; the scored bank holds one AP and one lateral of 148 cases (case134's two were missed by a folder walker) and scores case118's −58° oblique as a lateral; rebuilt at gate G0. No patient tag survives; the cases fall on five acquisition dates.
- (i) 692 public cases: 467 Elekta XVI (centres A–D), 225 Varian OBI (F–G); 152 Elekta cases were fetched before the analysis and 145 kept — two pilot cases (A004, A005) never entered the export list and five (A081, A239, A260, A303, B000) failed an axis-map check, B000 because its planning CT is released outside the imaged volume; 26 Varian cases trained no model; the full release is fetching (gate G0).
- (j) Totals over the additive rows: 4,093 renders (801 + 1,170 + 1,735 + 387); 9,108 level-bank films (4,963 + 3,603 + 542; 2,071 VinDr films and 950 FluoroCap panels sit in the fold banks alone); 4,276 real frames scored (296 + 366 + 3,480 + 116 + 18), of which the mobile C-arm rows (296, 366, 116) give 778; subjects are not totalled because the units differ.
- (k) Corpus: rebuilt from the public release by the released exporters (the licences differ, so derived frames are not redistributed). Parameters: a fitted nine-term image-formation file for the device, with the frames it was fitted on and its median held-out R² over six folds against uncalibrated physics. Weights: the released weights were trained on it (level weights = the fold models of the intraoperative cross-validation; detector = the held-out-device detector trained on COBRA2026 and DeepFluoro real frames); corpus and weights ship at gate G7. Absent above, used by no trained component: the SpinalAI (8,358 machine-generated renders), AASCE (457) and anchor-crop (7,842) render banks, the SpineSAC phantom frames, Mendeley-716.
