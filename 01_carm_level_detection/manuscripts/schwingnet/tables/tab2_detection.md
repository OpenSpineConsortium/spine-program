CI = confidence interval; PR-AUC = area under the precision–recall curve; IoU = intersection over union. Matching is greedy one-to-one at IoU ≥ 0.5; intervals are 95 % percentile bootstraps of 2,000 resamples clustered by case (C-ArmLSpine), patient (COBRA2026) or specimen (DeepFluoro); "—" = not scored at that operating point or not computed. Design A casts the styled renders under one pose pool for every device parameterisation, design B under each device's own poses.

- (a) Test sets: C-ArmLSpine 56 frames, 28 cases, 287 vertebrae, 56 sacra; COBRA2026 648 frames, 27 patients, 893 vertebrae, 609 frames with a sacrum; DeepFluoro six specimens, 366 frames, 430 vertebrae, 338 frames with a sacrum, the test specimen alone 104 frames, 83 vertebrae, 90 with a sacrum; plain radiographs 25 NHANES II films, 25 spines, 233 vertebral bodies. Truth boxes: C-ArmLSpine rows are scored against whole-vertebra outline boxes projected from the CBCT labels, the convention every number on disk uses; under the human-annotation convention (SPINEPS vertebral-body boxes) the reported detector reads 5.2 % (15/287) at 0.25 by convention mismatch, and that convention is the one of the pending flagship rows. COBRA2026 and DeepFluoro rows are scored against whole-vertebra boxes projected from the registered CT. A device is held out unless the row says control.
- (b) 0.74 = the best pooled vertebra F1 of the reported detector over its sweeps on COBRA2026, DeepFluoro and the Geneva phantom (`a1.json` `how_chosen`); no C-ArmLSpine frame enters the rule, which was adopted as the headline after the 0.25 result had been reported (REVIEW_STATS F16); 0.25 = the software default; the reported detector chose its checkpoint on the COBRA2026 and DeepFluoro test splits.
- (c) Non-interpolated average precision over the confidence sweep, computed for the design-B arms and the controls only.
- (d) Controls trained on the target device's own frames (test cases held out by case); on C-ArmLSpine the outline convention penalises these body-trained arms, which read 89.9 % (258/287) and 90.6 % (260/287) at 0.25 under body boxes.
- (e) One specimen is one cluster and carries no interval (the frame-level interval printed here resamples frames of one specimen and understates the uncertainty); the six-specimen rows are tests for arms that never saw the device, and their intervals rest on six clusters and are descriptive (percentile bootstraps over six units undercover).
- (f) Scored at 0.05 and 0.25 only; the film-trained recall is not a held-out number; the paired difference at 0.05 is +27.5 percentage points [20.5, 35.2] {20.9, 35.6}, sign test 23 vs 0 spines (exact P = 2.4e-7, Holm-adjusted 7.2e-7, `stats_review/recompute.json#R3_tests`), and at 0.25 +55.4 [47.0, 63.6] {46.5, 63.4}, 25 vs 0.
