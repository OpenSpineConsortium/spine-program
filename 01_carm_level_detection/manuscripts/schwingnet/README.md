# SchwingNet — the focused manuscript

The Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (Original Research) manuscript assembled on 2026-09-22 from the rigor
audit (`docs/RESUME_2026-09-22_PM.md`) and the foundation reports in this folder. It carries **only numbers
that pass the rigor audit**: an interval clustered at the case, specimen, patient or fold; a stated operating
point; and a result file it is read from. Every number in the text, tables and figures is listed with that file
in `NUMBERS.md` (the traceability rule). Nothing is quoted from a bank with a known split leak (allspine v2, v6,
v7; v8 for VerSe; anchor_drr; vindrps), from the one-specimen DeepFluoro test column without saying so, or from
`~/verse_drr` millimetres. The counting heuristic evaluated in `docs/ANOMALY_RULE_EVALUATION.md` does not
appear anywhere in this folder.

**State since 2026-09-24 (evening).** Paper 1 is the five-figure, three-table manuscript titled *SchwingNet: a
convolutional-recurrent network for level naming in spine surgery*. The LevelCheck /
surgeon comparison (former Figure 6, former Table 3 `tab_levelcheck`, the frozen comparator `LEVELCHECK_MODEL.md`, the

## How to build

Full TeX Live (the template's assumption): `latexmk` in this folder (xelatex + biber; `latexmkrc` drives it),
once for `main.tex` and once for `supplement.tex`. Times New Roman is selected through fontspec; on a machine
without it write a one-line `localfont.tex` holding `\setmainfont{<a serif font>}`.

Compute grid: `sbatch -D ~/logs ~/spinesurg-ct-nnunet/SchwingNet/slurm/compile.sh` after
`rsync -a --exclude results/inputs --exclude __pycache__ SchwingNet/ go2432@grid.wayne.edu:~/spinesurg-ct-nnunet/SchwingNet/`.
The script loads the `texlive/2020` module (xelatex, latexmk, biber, biblatex, endfloat, lwarp, glossaries),
writes `localfont.tex` (TeX Gyre Termes, since Times New Roman is absent), runs `latexmk -xelatex` on `main.tex`
and then on `supplement.tex`, and prints the page count of each; the log lands in
`~/logs/schwingnet_tex-<jobid>.out`. Without the module the nodes carry only TeX Live 2013 (no xelatex, no
biblatex), for which `rsnastyle.tex` holds a natbib/bibtex fallback. **Compile status is recorded at the end
of this file.**

No TeX engine is installed on the Windows workstation (`where xelatex` finds nothing); pages are rendered
from the PDFs with `tools/render_pdf.py` (PyMuPDF).

## Rules the folder was built under

- Only rigor-passing numbers (above). Both C-ArmLSpine cross-validation groupings are reported (case-grouped
  five-fold, acquisition-day-grouped three-fold, A9), the abstention operating point sits beside every naming
  headline and every headline says "at full coverage" (A7), the detector is quoted at a pre-stated operating
  point with the full curve (A1), every comparison is paired with the interval of the difference and
  Holm-adjusted (A3), the multiplicity is stated in Methods (A2), the ceiling interval is given as BCa (A4),
  film intervals are patient-clustered on the clean bank (A5), the device-card criterion is evaluated as
  written per fold (A6), and no verse_drr millimetre is printed (A8).
- The banned word never appears; captions are at most 60 words; numbers under ten are spelled out in prose;
  figures are sans-serif with grids; degrees use the symbol; every figure has at most four components.
- No commercial product and no regulatory pathway is named.
- The detector is drawn and described with the two classes the reported detectors have (vertebra, sacrum).
