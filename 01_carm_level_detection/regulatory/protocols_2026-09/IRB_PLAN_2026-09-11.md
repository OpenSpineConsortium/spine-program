# IRB plan, in order

Decision: two NEW protocols, not an amendment. Dr. Vaidya's IRB-21-10-4123 is the template
(retrospective chart review, Exempt Category 4, waiver of consent and of HIPAA authorization,
coded data on DMC Citrix) but a new protocol is the only way to write in, from day one, the
right to release de-identified images under a restricted data use agreement and to release
model weights, and to keep the data on the WSU cluster for your own research. Protocol 1 is
the retrospective build; Protocol 2 is the prospective silent evaluation, submitted at the
same time so it is approved by the time the models exist. Both go to WSU and to DMC (Dr.
Montie) on the same day. Amendment 47 on Dr. Vaidya's protocol can proceed for the students
regardless; it does not carry this work. Deadline: a grant supporting this work goes in by the
end of October 2026 and needs an approved IRB behind it, so the DMC side (Dr. Montie) is the
item to push; Dr. Edelman (department chair, faculty sponsor) and Dr. Dunbar (dean-level
signatory) can move the WSU side quickly.

Your WSU determination 2026 269 covers public data only and says DMC data needs DMC
authorization and HIPAA handling; that is what the new protocols and the DMC applications supply.

## Kept out on purpose

Anything shown to a treating clinician, any change to care, and any patient contact. Protocol 2
runs silently and is compared with the record afterward; the day a result is shown to a
clinician is a third protocol with its own consent discussion. Keeping both of these
research-only is what keeps them exempt and expedited.
