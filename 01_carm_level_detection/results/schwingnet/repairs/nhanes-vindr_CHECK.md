## 6. The box convention — **PASS on the conclusion, with two caveats**

Read `tools/nhanes_box_convention.py`, then looked at the renders rather than the numbers.

`L00160_masks.png`: red mask outlines are four- and five-corner quadrilaterals sitting on the vertebral
**bodies**, T12 through S1, with the posterior elements — pedicles, facets, the long lumbar spinous
processes, all plainly visible on the film — **outside** every outline. `C00160_masks.png`: C2 through
C6, bodies only, C2's polygon extended upward to take in the odontoid. The blue axis-aligned boxes are
therefore body boxes. The premise behind the standing convention holds for NHANES.

Caveat 1: the tool's own automatic verdict on this data is **not** "body". Its rule is
`wh < 1.6 and fill > 0.7` → body; the measured values are wh 1.01 and fill 0.54, which falls to the
`else` branch — *"ambiguous on shape alone (w/h 1.01, fill 0.54); read the renders"*. The audit text is
honest about this (it says the fill median "is not evidence about the convention" and that the renders
settle it), but the headline "measured, not assumed" rests on the renders, not on the shape test.

Caveat 2: the "about 2.5 for a whole vertebra" comparator is an anatomical estimate written in the
docstring (body depth ~35 mm, body + spinous ~85 mm). No whole-vertebra mask was measured. It is a
reasonable outside anchor and it is stated as one, but it is not a measurement.

Scope is stated correctly: established for NHANES only, not for BUU-LSPINE or VinDr.
