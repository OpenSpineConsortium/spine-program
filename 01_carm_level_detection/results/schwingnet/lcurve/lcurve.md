## Design

- Stage 1 = the design-B leave-one-device-out arm for the device (styled renders of the OTHER two devices on their own geometries + those devices' real train frames x2; the device itself contributed nothing). Stage 2 = full-depth fine-tuning (no frozen layer, SGD lr0 0.005 -> x0.05, 1 warm-up epoch, 20 epochs, no early stopping, the same augmentation as devcorpus_train.py) on: the stage-1 list + the device's OWN styled renders (its geometry, its card) + the N real frames repeated so that they are ~15 % of the sampled epoch. N = 0 is that fine-tune with no real frames.
- random.Random(seed) shuffles the pool of train cases (transitional cases removed first); for each target in increasing order the walk over that order adds a case whenever it brings the frame count closer to the target, continuing from the previous target's subset, so subsets are nested and whole cases are drawn. Realized N = the resulting frame count. N=0 = the stage-2 list with no real frames of the device.
- Checkpoint: best.pt by the device's real VAL split (fixed at every N, not counted in N; transitional case42 removed). Test = the device's realxray test split, never in any list.
- Transitional frames: the three real L6 patients of C-ArmLSpine (case42, case121, case136) are removed from EVERY list of EVERY device (the other devices' stage-1 lists carried case121 through the C-ArmLSpine real train split; those entries are dropped in stage 2) and scored as their own row with every C-ArmLSpine model. The x4 repetition for transitional cases inside a subset is implemented but applies to nothing here, since no known transitional case is in any pool.
- Second seed (subset and training seed both change) at N = 100 and at the top reachable N.

## Caveats

- COBRA's 24 frames per case are one CBCT sweep of one patient: N = 48 is two patients. DeepFluoro's case is a cadaveric specimen (24-111 frames), its test split is ONE specimen (18-1109), so its case-level interval does not exist and the frame-level one printed beside it understates the uncertainty.
- conf 0.74 was chosen for the C-ArmLSpine-held-out real-only arm on the other devices (a1.json) and is applied unchanged to every model here; it is a pre-stated point, not a tuned one.
- The stage-1 arms selected best.pt on the other devices' validation frames; every stage-2 cell selects on the device's own val split. The all-real arms selected on their own val (and lodo_carm on the COBRA / DeepFluoro TEST splits, as flagged).
- A C-ArmLSpine number under the body convention (SPINEPS body boxes) is also in lcurve.json; the outline convention is quoted because the render boxes are whole vertebrae.
