## Definitions and bins

- Convention: tools/pose_arc.py module docstring (lat 0 = AP source posterior, -90 = real Surgio lateral, +90 = RAO family; tilt + cranial; roll + up toward patient left).
- lat: 18 bins of 10 deg over -90..+90 (pose_arc lat: 0 = AP (source posterior), -90 = real Surgio lateral, +90 = RAO family). |lat| > 90 (the PA family) is counted OUTSIDE and never binned.
- tilt: 6 bins of 10 deg over -30..+30 (+ = beam toward the head).
- roll: 12 bins of 5 deg over -30..+30 (+ = image-up toward the patient's left; folded by 180 (stored-upside-down frames)).
- mag: 16 bins of 0.05 over 1.20..2.00, M = SDD/SOD.
- field: 8 bins of 50 mm over 100..500 mm, exposed cranio-caudal field / M.
- joint: lat x tilt, K = 108.
- position: supine, prone, other, not_recorded, from DICOM PatientPosition (0018,5100) only.
- A bin is occupied at >= 1 item. For a corpus every item counts once. For a training set H* is over epoch ENTRIES (a frame repeated x46 in the list is x46 of the epoch) and C over unique items; `n_outside` counts entries beyond the clinically relevant range, `n_unreadable` those with no value.

## D. What could not be read

- **COBRA2026 Varian projections: lat, tilt**: the RTK-world -> patient-frame axis map was never solved for Varian (realxray_cobra_export excludes them); gantry angles, SID, SDD and imager size are read and reported
- **PatientPosition**: present only in C-ArmLSpine headers (and on a subset of them); DeepFluoro's h5, COBRA's metadata.yaml, the renderers and the phantom set record none
- **Detector roll of real frames**: no C-ArmLSpine header carries a detector or table rotation tag; roll is known only where a registration read it back (296 spot poses, the case134 spin)
- **Geneva hip magnification**: the export meta records no SDD/SOD for the BV Pulsera phantom frames
- **Parallel-projection renders (rare_class totalsegmentator_drr, verse_drr): M, source side**: a parallel projection has no source; AP/PA and left/right laterals are the same image
- **Crop banks of the naming heads**: a crop bank records no per-crop pose; see training_sets[*].joinable = false
- **Plain-film corpora (NHANES, BUU, CSXA, VinDr, AASCE, lat236, Mendeley, Spinal-AI)**: radiographs with no positioner, SID or field tags on disk; only a view protocol (AP or lateral) is known. Listed in the report from docs/XRAY_DATASET_USAGE.json
- **The style stage's rot-180 draw**: a stored flip, not a change of the C-arm pose; not counted as roll
