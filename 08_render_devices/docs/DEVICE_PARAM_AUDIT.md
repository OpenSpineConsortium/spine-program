# Where every C-arm parameter comes from, and whether a render draws it

Generated by `tools/device_param_audit.py` from the cards in `docs/devices/` and the DICOM header census in
`docs/devices/census/`. Nothing here is written by hand: if a card changes, this file changes with it.

## The answer in one paragraph

A styled frame is a DRAW, not a best fit. Of 19 physical parameters across 6 devices, 51 are sampled per frame from the device's fitted Gaussian copula, conditioned on the patient's water-equivalent thickness through that device's own automatic-exposure regression; 54 are held at the device's fitted median; 5 fall back to a physics default because the device has no paired frames for them. 9 parameter values are read from DICOM headers rather than inferred from the image, and 4 are harvested into the card but not consumed by the renderer -- those are named below rather than left for a reader to find.

Every per-frame draw is clipped to the card's central band (`device_style.SPREAD = 0.2`), so the sampler reaches 60% of each fitted marginal and not its tails. That is a deliberate choice with a cost, and the cost is measured in `slurm/device_spread_sweep.sh` rather than asserted here.
