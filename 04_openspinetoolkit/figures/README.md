# figures/

## `student_panels_2026-05/`

Six figure panels of 2026-05-18 from the first round of student miniprojects, before ostk existed:
`Cobb.png` (coronal Cobb), `LDI.png` (lordosis distribution index), `TRALL.png` (TRALL lumbar lordosis),
`VWI.png` (vertebral wedging index), `Tortuosity.jpg` (centroid-trajectory tortuosity) and
`Figure1_OSC_measurement_panels.png` (the composite). They match the miniproject names in ostk's
`projects/README.md` and were made with the pre-ostk scripts in `../code/pre_ostk_2026-05/` on the May 2026
sample export (legacy label map). Record only: no number in them is quotable, and none of them is a figure of
any manuscript in this programme. The two files that sat beside them in the source folder, a participation
survey CSV and an abstract-portal PDF, were not copied because they carry names and addresses
(`../MANIFEST_LOCAL.md`, "not copied").

Captions, if any of these is ever reused: at most 60 words, the argument in the text (`../CLAUDE.md` rule 1).
