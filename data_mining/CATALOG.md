# Fluoroscopy Data Atlas: catalog

Every fluoroscopy, spine radiograph, paired CT and X-ray, CBCT projection and DRR dataset found for intraoperative vertebral level identification, as of 17 October 2026, with what is already on the grid and how to get the rest. This file is the same records as [`fluoroscopy_data_atlas.html`](fluoroscopy_data_atlas.html), which adds search and filters (download it and open it in a browser; [README.md](README.md) says how).

**Status**: *held* means present under `/rs/rs_grp_oschome/go2432/data/` on the Wayne State grid, readable by approved students; *request ready* means a letter is drafted; *not held* means you do not have it. **Flags**: *levels named* (spinal levels are labelled), *pose known* (projection geometry links each X-ray to its CT or to the C-arm), *link not opened* (from search results only).

## Worth getting next

1. **COVID-19-NY-SBU (CT plus CR/DX)**. *open download*. You already hold the CTs of 439 patients. Fetching their CR/DX radiographs from the same TCIA collection gives same-patient CT and X-ray pairs with no request at all. [page](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/covid-19-ny-sbu/) [paper](https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.BBAG-2923)
2. **LUMOS lumbar osteoporosis dataset (CT + radiographs)**. *open download*. 1,620 real lumbar AP and lateral films in DICOM, with 280 lumbar CTs from the same cohort. Open download, CC BY-NC. Which film goes with which CT is not stated. [page](https://zenodo.org/records/18173664) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746027.3758282)
3. **MIDRC Open Data Commons**. *free registration*. 10,100 living cases with both CT and a radiograph of the same patient, about 300 of them spine radiograph studies. Free registration and a click-through agreement. No pose. [page](https://www.midrc.org/midrc-data)
4. **Balgrist ex-vivo lumbar CT + C-arm set (Jecklin et al.)**. *request to authors*. 13 soft-tissue torsos, about 1,000 real shots on a Siemens Cios Spin and two Ziehm C-arms, with poses from implanted fiducials. The best fit that exists; request letter 15 is ready. [page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16027)
5. **UCLA kV/MV setup radiographs with planning CT (Charters et al.)**. *request to authors*. 254 living patients, 2,486 orthogonal kV pairs of the thoraco-abdominal spine registered to the planning CT. Request only; the authors call the data sensitive. [page](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2057-1976/ad2baa) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1088/2057-1976/ad2baa)
6. **PENGWIN Task 2 DeepDRR renders**. *open download*. 50,000 unwindowed DRRs in C-arm geometry with simulated K-wires and screws and sacrum masks. CC BY 4.0, 35 GB. Tests robustness to surgical hardware. [page](https://zenodo.org/records/10913196) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-43996-4_30)
7. **SyntheX deposit (JHU Dataverse 2PGJQU)**. *open download*. Simulated hip images matched in geometry to real C-arm frames from two systems. A benchmark for your own render physics. Open, CC BY-NC. [page](https://doi.org/10.7281/T1/2PGJQU) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-023-00629-1)
8. **SPARE (Sydney)**. *open download*. Real Varian and Elekta kV projections with per-projection geometry and a prior 4D-CT. Open, CC BY-NC; you register the CT to the projections yourself. [page](https://hdl.handle.net/2123/34378) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/mp.13687)
9. **LIDC-IDRI (CT plus chest radiographs)**. *open download*. 290 patients with CT and a chest radiograph of the same patient. Open on TCIA. [page](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/lidc-idri/) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1118/1.3528204)
10. **Femur multi-view X-ray/CT dataset (Flepp et al.)**. *open download*. Five cadaveric distal femurs with CT and fiducial-calibrated X-rays from a Siemens Fluorospot Compact. Downloadable now under a research-only licence; no spine. [page](https://zenodo.org/records/15753063) [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-025-03391-4)

## Requests to send

Sixteen sources can only be had by asking. The letters, with what to expect from each, are in [FLUORO_VIDEO_REQUESTS_READY_2026-09-26.md](../01_carm_level_detection/docs/data_requests/FLUORO_VIDEO_REQUESTS_READY_2026-09-26.md) and, for Balgrist and SX-Stitch, beside it in the same folder. Eleven also exist as unsent Outlook drafts in `spinesurg-ct-nnunet/docs/fluoro_requests_eml/` on Greg's laptop. The HTML page carries every letter with a copy button.

**Radiopaedia (request 8).** Seven multi-frame spine fluoroscopy cases, among them rID 24796 (a rotational C-arm spin), rID 90103 (a 61-projection oblique lumbar puncture series) and rID 21585 (a lateral L1 vertebroplasty series).

1. Open [radiopaedia.org/licence](https://radiopaedia.org/licence) in your own browser and read the current machine-learning terms.
2. File their application for non-commercial machine-learning use, or email the licensing team with letter 8. Published studies cite `license@radiopaedia.org` and an approval under "Non-commercial use of Radiopaedia content for Machine Learning"; confirm the address on the page.
3. Expect a non-refundable fee: the draft recorded USD 250, which could not be confirmed from public sources on 17 October 2026.
4. Save no frames before approval: their terms bar scripted downloading, and validating the 510(k) product would need a separately negotiated licence.
