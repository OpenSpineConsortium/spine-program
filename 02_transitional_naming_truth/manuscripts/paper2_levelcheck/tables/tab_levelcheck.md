L5, L6 = fifth, sixth lumbar vertebra; S1 = first sacral segment; T13 = thirteenth thoracic vertebra; LevelCheck = labelling by registration of the preoperative CT to the intraoperative radiograph (Otake 2012).

- (a) Model: LevelCheck carries the labels a reader assigned to the preoperative CT, so its accuracy on a stratum is r(1 − e), with r the registration success, frozen at 1.0 (published 0.993–1.000, Lo 2015), and e the probability that the planning read names a scored vertebra otherwise than the reference; no residual surgeon error is multiplied in, both simplifications favouring LevelCheck. Frozen by commit on 23 September 2026 before any held-out transitional person was scored; seen before the freeze were the ordinary-spine development results, the three C-ArmLSpine L6 patients and render L6 recall of 56.1–72.4 %, all below 82.2 % where transitional.
- (b) Membership from the reference standard alone, fixed before unblinding. At risk: a transitional person with a frame on which a reference-named body is misnamed by counting up from the sacrum over five lumbar vertebrae or down from C2 over seven cervical, twelve thoracic and five lumbar. Naming unaffected: Castellvi I/II, lumbar rib, a fused L6 left unlabelled, a sacralised L5 keeping its label, or no flagged frame. The three C-ArmLSpine L6 patients are excluded from the primary analysis in advance and reported descriptively.
- (c) Unit: the person. One frame with at least four reference-named bodies drawn per person by a fixed seed from the truth alone; correct when every reference-named body receives its reference name (missed, unnamed, abstained or unanchored = wrong). SchwingNet configuration fixed before unblinding: an arm never trained on a test person (encoder from ImageNet weights on the split's training persons), the coupled call, detector confidence 0.74, whole frame, seed 0. Reference: the source's reader truth; otherwise a human verification or, failing that, an automated reference whose rule and calibrated positive predictive value were frozen before unblinding (added by commit on 24 September 2026, with the secondary analyses of 23 September). One-sided exact binomial P, the probability of at least k correct of n at the frozen value; Holm over the eligible hypotheses at a familywise one-sided 0.025; Wilson 95 % interval for description.
- (d) The smallest n at which n of n correct gives P ≤ 0.025; a hypothesis whose stratum is smaller is reported descriptively and not claimed.
