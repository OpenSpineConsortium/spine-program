# CT-segmentation transitional-phenotype classifier — design note

Written 2026-09-24 (workflow `ct-lstv-classifier-design`, runId `wf_2db5e6f9-78f`); revised the same day after the
critic pass (§11 Revision log). Status: DESIGN ONLY. No job has been submitted; the job table in §8 is submitted only after
Greg approves the decisions in §10, one submission at a time, each writing ids + ETA to `docs/RESUME_2026-09-22_PM.md` and
stopping (submit-and-sleep rule). The first submission is now a 20-CT timing task (J0), because no per-CT cost for the new
feature block has been measured and the earlier wall-time figures were invented.

**What it is for.** Greg, 2026-09-24: "solve my current problem of having all of these segmented CTs with unverified
anomalies by using the existing known radiologist annotated anomalies from VerSeFusion / CTSpinoPelvic1K to train a
classifier on CT segmentation ... without requiring any manual human intervention and then using that to pseudo adjudicate
all these flagged scans". It is also product A of `CTSpinoPelvic1K-1/COMMERCIALIZATION.md` line 21 ("Flag suspected LSTV
on preoperative CT, notify reader ... Cleanest 510(k) path"). Intended-use sentence (draft, numbering-relevant anatomy
only): *the software flags CT examinations whose lumbosacral or thoracolumbar junction shows anatomy that changes vertebral
numbering (six or four lumbar-type vertebrae, a fused or pseudoarticulating last lumbar vertebra, 11 or 13 rib-bearing
vertebrae, a rib on the first lumbar vertebra) for review by a reader; it does not name levels, grade Castellvi type or
report a finding when the junction is outside the field of view.*

**The one-line verdict.** With the reads that exist today, exactly two things can be certified to the project's own
standard (Wilson 95 % lower bound on PPV >= 0.90 per subtype, `tools/auto_reference_calibrate.py` L57 `PPV_LB_MIN = 0.90`):
*ordinary* and *LS-L6 (six lumbar-type bodies, 25 presacral)*. The L6 pool, counted from the label source with one count per
record (`SchwingNet/lit/ct_lstv_classifier/positives_table.md`, generated by `positives_table.py`), is **93 read persons**
(CTSP 16, VerSe 76, CTSpine1K 1) and **88 after the five scan-contradicted VerSe reads are excluded**; LS-L6 certifies if the
classifier makes <= 3 errors on those 88 (LB 0.905), or 0 errors on >= 35. This verdict refers to the branch in which reader
E stays *outside* Figure 6 (decision 6a); if E enters the fusion, the pool is the in_calibration subset, **51-52 persons**,
and certification needs zero errors (LB 0.931 at 52, 0.899 with one error; §2, second table). Sacralised-fused L5 (**24**
read persons after the CTSP recount), Castellvi II (**28**), T13 (6), numerical four-lumbar (2 verified), lumbar rib (12
read), hypoplastic 12th rib (18) **cannot** reach the bound at current n even with zero errors (§2). They get a
high-sensitivity *candidate* tier and a stated PPV with its interval; they do not get an *adjudicated* tier until more reads
exist. Seven CTSP read records (0008 0021 0033 0049 0176 0179 0196) whose readers' own count disagrees with the release
count are **out of every lumbosacral truth pool until re-read** (§1a table B). Block vertebra and hemivertebra have zero
labelled examples anywhere and are out of scope except as an out-of-distribution flag.

## 3. Targets: phenotype, not label id

Two junction heads plus abstain, evaluated per person:

**Lumbosacral (LS) head** — classes are defined so that no spine satisfies two of them:
LS-0 ordinary (five lumbar-type, 24 presacral, no pseudarthrosis/fusion); **LS-L6 (six lumbar-type bodies = 25 presacral,
regardless of Castellvi grade** — a fused Castellvi III/IV sixth body is still LS-L6, so the 35 graded III/IV L6 of §1a are
one class, not two); **LS-SAC (five lumbar-type = 24 presacral, the last one fused or pseudo-fused to the sacrum, Castellvi
III/IV, 23 mobile)**; LS-II (five lumbar-type, pseudoarthrosis, Castellvi II); LS-4L (four lumbar-type, 23 presacral,
unfused); **LS-NA abstain** (sacral plateau or the last two lumbar bodies outside the FOV, the sacrum source missing, or
skeletal immaturity: iliac apophysis unfused or an open S1-S2 disc, so an unfused S1 cannot be counted as a free body).
Castellvi grade is a *field*, never a class, on every LS class where a read exists; stump/contact side and six-segment
sacrum likewise. A 24-presacral spine with a rib-free 20th body (the CTSP rib-convention spines and the VerSe 7/11/6/5
reads) is LS-0 on this head and TL-11 on the other; it is never LS-L6. Mapping into the fusion vocabulary of
`auto_reference_calibrate.py` `norm_call` L84 (ordinary / L6 / T13 / four_lumbar): LS-L6 -> L6 whatever its grade; LS-SAC
with the fused body merged into the sacrum id -> four_lumbar, with its own id -> ordinary (the calibration convention,
`CALIBRATION.md` L7); LS-II -> ordinary; LS-4L -> four_lumbar; LS-NA -> no_count.

**Thoracolumbar (TL) head** — TL-0 (12 rib-bearing bodies, last rib >= 38 mm on at least one side); TL-13 (13 rib-bearing);
TL-11 (11 rib-bearing, i.e. a rib-free 20th body); TL-LR (rib 12-38 mm on the body below the last long-rib body = lumbar
rib on L1, count unaffected); TL-H12 (12-38 mm rib on the 12th rib-bearing body = hypoplastic 12th rib, count unaffected);
**TL-NA abstain** (the last rib-bearing body, or the reference needed to count it, outside the FOV). Because no CTSP FOV
reaches T1, TL-13 versus TL-LR is decided by the relation to the sacrum where both junctions are in field (13 rib-bearing +
25 presacral = TL-13; 12 rib-bearing + a 12-38 mm rib on the 20th body = TL-LR); with the sacrum out of field (RibFrac) the
head emits the local descriptor only and abstains on the count.

**Out-of-distribution flag** (`unusual_spine`): raised, never classified, when per-body volume/spacing/HU leave the
training envelope (hardware ids 62-68 present, a 1.6x spacing gap = missing body, a squeezed pair = two bodies under one
name, a body with < 5 ml, a run of fused bodies) **or when the skeleton is immature** (open iliac apophysis on the hip
mask, an S1-S2 disc gap in the sacral occupancy profile, or DICOM age < 18 where it exists): the TS census already showed
12 of 26 four-lumbar/11-rib candidates are paediatric with an unfused S1 reading as a free body (`positives_census.md`
§5.1). Block vertebra, hemivertebra, instrumented and immature spines land here; with zero labelled examples they are not
targets.

Why phenotype rather than label id. (a) Segmenters *count*: the release, SPINEPS and TS each name L1..L5 by position and
disagree exactly on the anomalies (CTSP release names five lumbar on the two-labeller L6; SPINEPS puts the CTSP L6 in its
sacrum in 7/18; TS has no L6/T13 class at all, `README` 117 classes). (b) SPINEPS masks harvested before 147428d carry raw
ids 1..N and a raw-tree relabel carries no sacrum (`tools/spineps_ids.py`). (c) VERIDAH's own names against the readers: A
alone L6 PPV 0.750 (24/32), T13 0.333, four-lumbar 0.143; true L6 called ordinary 0.49 in the Dawid-Skene confusion
(`CALIBRATION.md`). (d) VERIDAH collapses every Castellvi anomaly to "sacralized lumbar vertebra regardless of the Castellvi
type" (arXiv 2601.14066). (e) The label id is what we are trying to *decide*; feeding it back as input is circular.

comes from whichever segmenter is being scored (release v11 / SPINEPS post-fix / TS `total`); the *names* the segmenter
assigns are dropped. Every feature is computed from the CT and the instance geometry so the same code runs on all three.

**Render the mask.** Every feature extractor writes the midsagittal + coronal PNG (`tools/transitional_mask_census.py`
already does) and the job's QC step renders 20 random training rows and every held/contradicted row before any number is
believed. This is the third time in the project that a render settled what numbers did not.

**Model.** Gradient-boosted trees (LightGBM/XGBoost, monotone constraints where physics says so: more rib length -> more
thoracic) over a fixed-width vector built from the per-instance table: the last 8 instances above the sacral plateau and the
4 around the last rib-bearing body, each with its features, plus case-level counts and FOV flags; missing = explicit NaN
with a mask bit. A second arm is a small bidirectional GRU over the variable-length per-instance sequence (2 layers, 64
units, ~50 k parameters). Both output per-head class probabilities and a **reason string** assembled from the top SHAP
features in plain words ("6 rib-free bodies above a sacral plateau at 240 mm; last rib 41 mm right / 12 mm left; TP-ala
contact 0 mm2 both sides").

Why not a 3-D CNN at this n: the positive classes have 2-103 persons; a 3-D CNN on a 100-mm junction crop would have to
learn rib tracing, disc detection and TP-ala contact from ~30 examples of each, cannot be run identically on three
segmenters (it would see the mask), and gives no reason string. The features above are the quantities the readers
themselves use (Castellvi 1984 morphology; the 38 mm rib rule of Liebl 2021 / Moller 2026; the vertebra-shape rules of
Schinz 2026), each with an existing implementation and its documented traps. A **CNN arm is reserved** for one job: the
TP-ala contact patch (a 40 x 40 x 40 mm crop per side around the lowest lumbar TP tip), trained only if the tabular LS-II /
LS-SAC separation is insufficient on validation, and only after the read pool for those classes has grown (§2).

## 5. Training and validation protocol

- **Persons, not scans.** Key by `tools/patient_keys.py` `key_any`/`canon_key`; aliases merged first (VerSe 18 persons
  with > 1 scan; five subjects with two series; 11 VerSe scans redistributed in CTSpine1K; 782 CTSpine1K COLONOG volumes are
  CTSP persons). CTSpine1K oblique stems and `ctspine1k_<stem>` are not in `SCHEMES` yet: key on the ann's `patient_key`.
- **Folds.** StratifiedGroupKFold, K = 5, grouped by person, stratified on the joint (LS class, TL class); repeated with 5
  seeds (0..4). Out-of-fold predictions pooled once per seed; intervals reported over the pooled set with the seed spread
  beside them. Thresholds (tau per class) chosen *inside* each training fold by an inner 4-fold on the training persons only
  (nested); the outer fold never sees its own tau being tuned. Machinery copied from `tools/lstv_flag_train.py` /
  `lstv_flag_eval.py` (patient-grouped 5-fold, OOF pooling, tau + abstention).
- **Truth.** Read tier only (§1a), phenotype from the label source never from a segmenter's names, **one count source per
  record**: on CTSP the readers' own count (`Lumbosac.csv`) must equal the release count or the record is out of LS truth
  (table B: 7 records to re-read); on VerSe the read's `assumed_segment_structure` decides LS-L6 (25 presacral) versus TL-11
  (7/11/6/5). Contradicted reads (§1b) are excluded from that phenotype's truth and counted in a label-noise table. CTSP 738
  non-row records enter as *presumed ordinary* for the count classes with a flag; they never enter LS-II truth.
- **Persons on the G3 test side.** Under decision 6a (recommended) reader E trains on every read-tier person, including
  the G3 test side (auto_truth.csv test-side L6: CTSP 7 + VerSe 33; per-subtype test sides in `split_plan.md` L20-35).
  That is only safe because of the bar in §7: **nothing reader E produces (`adjudicated_auto` or `candidate`) may enter any
  bank whose descendants score Figure 6, the LODO arms or any G3 test person**, and `checkpoint_lineage` is extended so
  the bar is checked by machine, not by memory (§7). Under 6b E excludes every G3 test person from training *and*
  validation (pool = the 945 in_calibration persons, §2 second table) and the frozen reference is re-frozen under a new sha.
- **Segmenter invariance in training.** Each training person contributes one feature row per available segmenter
  (release / SPINEPS / TS), all in the same fold (grouping is by person). At evaluation, a person's call is the call on the
  *primary* segmenter for that collection with the others used as the invariance check (§6 ii), not as extra votes.
- **G3 / Figure 6.** This classifier does **not** feed Figure 6 (recommendation): Figure 6's automated reference is frozen
  (`SchwingNet/results/fig6/frozen/auto_reference.sha256`, `--freeze` refuses overwrite) and its calibration set is the 945
  in_calibration persons (CTSP 707 + VerSe 238, 0 G3 test). Keeping the classifier out of Figure 6 frees it from the G3
  constraint for its *own* training, but two things are kept: (a) the G3 test persons (`split_final.json` sha256
  65a57eb0..., per-subtype test sides L6 43, T13 3, sacralised 11, castellvi_I_II 12, lumbar_rib 6) are recorded per fold so
  that a later decision to feed Figure 6 can be audited; (b) its own held-out persons pass `tools/eval_gate.py assert_unseen`
  (L662) semantics: every scored person keyed, lineage recorded in `touched.json`, `--not-held-out "<reason>"` the only
  bypass. If Greg instead wants the classifier *as reader E inside Figure 6's fusion*, then its training set must exclude
  every G3 test person and the frozen reference must be re-frozen under a new sha — decision 6.
- **COBRA and C-ArmLSpine are TEST only** (real frames): scored by the census, never in any fold.
- **Reported per phenotype and per head:** PPV, sensitivity, specificity with Wilson 95 % intervals; **coverage beside
  accuracy** (selective prediction: coverage = share of persons with a non-abstain call; a table of PPV at coverage 100 / 90 /
  80 / 70 %); the Dawid-Skene-style confusion (true class x called class); calibration (reliability diagram, ECE, per class);
  the label-noise table; per-collection results separately (CTSP vs VerSe — the fusion's L6 PPV is 1.000 on VerSe and 0.538
  on CTSP for a reason the classifier must not inherit).
- **Certification.** The §2 rule is applied to the pooled OOF calls: a phenotype enters the adjudicated tier only if its
  PPV LB >= 0.90 in *every* seed. Expected at launch: ordinary and L6.

## 6. Outside checks (nothing is believed from internal consistency)

(i) **Reader E in the Dawid-Skene fusion.** Plug-in points: `tools/auto_reference_calibrate.py` `READERS` L53 and
`load_readers` L115; `DawidSkene` L228, `metrics` L313, `qualifies` L331. Reader E's per-person call (LS head x TL head
collapsed to the reader vocabulary: ordinary / L6 / T13 / four_lumbar, with sacralised_fused -> four_lumbar per
`norm_call`, cut field -> no_count) is added and the three rules rerun on the 945-person calibration set. **Adjudicated
tier = reader E + at least one independent route agreeing**: rib count (reader C, direct CT trace) first; counting from C2
(reader A's ctd sequence with `t1` anchored) where the whole spine is in field; reader B third. Disagreement between E and
every route is a flag, not a vote. The classifier is not allowed to see the reader A/B/C calls as features (it may reuse
their *geometry*, §4), otherwise the routes are not independent.

(ii) **Segmenter invariance.** The same CT segmented by TotalSegmentator and by SPINEPS (and the release where it exists)
must receive the same phenotype from reader E. Disagreement -> `segmenter_disagreement=True`, person drops to candidate
tier regardless of score. Reported as an agreement rate per collection with its Wilson interval **over every CT with all
segmenters available (CTSP: all 802), and separately over the 27 CTSP records where release and SPINEPS already disagree
on the count and over the read positives** — the earlier plan computed it on the 775 pre-selected for agreement, which is
circular; the interesting number is the disagreement rate on the positives (SPINEPS counts five on 7 of the 18 CTSP read
L6, `ctsp/rows.csv` labeller_b).

(iii) **Census share inside the published prevalence** (verified sources only; §9). **Per class, per collection, on the
denominator of persons with that class's junction and reference in field and the skeleton mature** (LS classes need the
sacral plateau and the last rib-bearing body; TL classes need T1 or C7 as well). Windows and the stop rule per class:

| class | window (adult, unselected) | source | stop rule (halts J6 on that class and collection) |
|---|---|---|---|
| LS-L6 | 3.2-8.2 % | Doo 2020 lumbarisation 3.2 % whole-spine CT; Nagata 2025 4.0 %; Sabri 2025 5.5 % 25 presacral; Paik 2013 8.2 % six lumbar (MRI) | < 0.5 % or > 40 % |

the reason written in the census: paediatric scans (TS: age gate removes them into LS-NA), fracture-enriched RibFrac
(Moller's 20.5 % stump-rib share is a fracture cohort; the sacrum is out of field on 651/660 anyway), single-sex oncology
sets (deep_psma: men; L6/25-presacral skews male, LS-4L/23 skews female, `lit_prevalence_and_definitions.md` L326-328),
and lumbar-only CBCT (C-ArmLSpine). Where DICOM sex exists the LS-L6 and LS-4L shares are reported by sex against those
directions. The present two-labeller census sits **inside** the LS-L6 window on CTSP (18+10 of 802 = 3.5 %, window 3.2-8.2 %)
and below it on TS (22/815 = 2.7 %) and COBRA (3/692 = 0.4 %, but 497 of 692 have no count), which says something about
the current readers' sensitivity on those collections, not that the collections are unusual.

(iv) **The one optional human step.** A spot check by a reader who has not seen the masks, drawn stratified: **35 from the
adjudicated tier (25 LS-L6, 10 ordinary) plus 10 from the candidate tier's LS-SAC/LS-II** (the ten that would move those
classes toward certification, §2) = 45 reads. With 35 zero-error adjudicated-tier reads the tier's transported PPV has LB
0.901 (one error 0.855); the earlier "40 reads -> LB 0.912" counted the 10 candidate-tier reads toward the adjudicated tier,
which they cannot certify (30 zero-error reads give 0.886 < 0.90). If only 40 reads are affordable, the spot check does not
by itself certify the tier and the paper says so. **If it is skipped the paper must say:** "the adjudicated tier's positive predictive value was cross-validated on the read
set (CTSP 33 records read by two radiology residents with consensus; VerSe 325 subjects read by two neuroradiologists in
consensus) and transported to the census without a new human read; the census tiers are therefore pseudo-labels whose
error rate is the cross-validated one, not a measured one, and the inter-reader ceiling for Castellvi typing on CT is
kappa 0.63-0.71 (Hanhivaara 2024)."

## 7. Pseudo-adjudication tiers, registry fields, and the flow into the harvest

Tiers (per person, per head):
- **adjudicated** — reader E class is a certified class (§2/§5), score >= tau_class, segmenter-invariant (§6 ii), and >= 1
  independent route agrees (§6 i). Trains downstream models **subject to the leak bar below** (never a bank whose
  descendants score Figure 6, the LODO arms or a G3 test person, under decision 6a).
- **candidate** — reader E class at the high-sensitivity tau (chosen on inner folds for sensitivity >= 0.95 where the pool
  allows), or a non-certified class at any tau, or an adjudicated call that failed invariance/route agreement. Maximises
  examples; enters banks only with `truth_tier=candidate` and never as a Figure 6 or LODO truth.
- **flag_disagreement** — E disagrees with every route, or segmenters disagree: queued for a reader.
- **abstain** — LS-NA / TL-NA, or `unusual_spine`.
- **read** — unchanged: a physician read exists; E's call is recorded beside it for the agreement table, never overrides it.

Registry fields added to `SchwingNet/results/transitional_registry/registry.csv` (existing schema: `patient_key, is_person,
in_streams, subtype, call, truth_tier, castellvi, ..., labeller_a, labeller_b, agree, n_thoracic_rib_bearing, n_lumbar,
junction_tl_in_fov, junction_ls_in_fov, fused_mask_representation, review_queue, notes`): `reader_e_ls, reader_e_tl,
reader_e_p_ls, reader_e_p_tl, reader_e_tau_tier, reader_e_reason, reader_e_segmenters (which masks scored),
reader_e_invariant, reader_e_routes_agreeing (subset of {A,B,C,C2count}), reader_e_tier, reader_e_model_sha,
reader_e_features_sha, unusual_spine`. `truth_tier` gains the value `adjudicated_auto`; existing `read` and `two-labeller`
values are untouched. Per-case JSON with the full feature row and reason lands at
`/rs/rs_grp_oschome/go2432/data/or_ready/auto_reference/readerE/<corpus>/json/<case>.json` beside readers A/B/C, with a
render PNG per case. A `REGISTRY.md` paragraph records model sha, feature sha, tau per class, the certification table and
the per-collection prevalence check.

Flow into the harvest, **with the leak bar**. `tools/rare_class_maximize.py` reads `truth_tier` (it has no tier flag today:
its argparse L117-123 takes `--registry/--out/--rendered-json/--scan-roots/--before-frames/--jobs/--after-dir`; a new
`--tier {read,adjudicated_auto,candidate}` is added). Rules:
- `adjudicated_auto` is admitted beside `read` **only into banks whose descendants never score Figure 6, the LODO arms or
  any G3 test person**. Under decision 6a reader E has seen G3 test persons' CTs and reads, so a pseudo-label it produces
  is a G3 test label in disguise; `rare_class_maximize.py --tier adjudicated_auto` refuses to write into any root listed
  in `SchwingNet/results/fig6/frozen/` or named by a LODO arm, and writes `label_source_models` into every ann it emits.
- `candidate` is admitted to the *candidate* bank only (`--tier candidate`), which is what the harvest directive asks for
  (maximise examples) while the trained-on bank stays `read` (+ `adjudicated_auto` where the bar allows).
- **Lineage covers label sources, not only weights.** `tools/checkpoint_lineage.py` `touched.json` today records
  `train_keys / val_keys` over the weight-initialisation chain (L49, L102-105) and `eval_gate.assert_unseen` (L662) reads
  only that; a bank pseudo-labelled by a model that saw the test persons passes it. Change: every ann and every bank
  manifest carries `label_source_models: [{model_sha, features_sha, train_keys_sha, val_keys_sha}]`; `checkpoint_lineage`
  unions those models' train/val keys into `touched_keys` of any checkpoint trained on the bank; `assert_unseen` therefore
  raises `SeenInEvaluation` when a Figure 6 / LODO / G3 test person is among reader E's training or validation keys. This
  is the v5-pseudolabel failure (19 points of anomalous accuracy) made impossible by construction rather than by
  discipline.
The held lists (`maximize/after/held_all.json`, `waiting.csv`) are re-emitted with E's call so a reader sees the
classifier's opinion next to the two labellers'. `tools/make_split.py` continues to put every real-frame transitional
person on the TEST side; nothing here changes the split.

## 9. Literature position (verified citations only)

No peer-reviewed CT-based LSTV classifier validated against radiologist Castellvi reads exists as of 2026-09-24
(PubMed and Europe PMC searches, `lit/ct_lstv_classifier/lit_ct_lstv_automation.md`). The only peer-reviewed deep-learning
LSTV detector is on standing lateral radiographs: Kwak et al. 2025, binary ResNet-50, test accuracy 76.4 %, sensitivity
85.1 %, specificity 61.9 %, AUC 0.84 on 313 films, single site, no Castellvi subtyping, below the one orthopaedic reader
(83.9 %) [PMID 41227066]. On CT, what exists is enumeration-anomaly *labelling*: Hanaoka et al. 2017 chose among five
count hypotheses on 300 neck-to-pelvis CTs (normal 211/217; count anomaly without a transitional vertebra 9/10; transitional
71/73 "partially" — undefined in the abstract, full text paywalled) [PMID 28063076]; VERIDAH (preprint, arXiv 2601.14066)
labels every vertebra correctly in 99.18 % of 365 in-house CTs with lumbar-anomaly recall 97.22 % and thoracic 96.30 %
(the paper states percentages only; the denominators 36 and 27 are inferred from the percentage grid, `CITABLE_CLAIMS.md`
row 56, so write k/36 and k/27, never "35 of 36"), against 77.81 % / 61.11 % / 11.11 % for the graph labeller of Meng et
al., but it has no L6/T13 class in its head, one 3-year annotator, no external test and no Castellvi type. The VerSe
benchmark showed why counting fails on these spines: no VerSe'19 method identified the one hidden L6; after enrichment to
47 L6 and 6 T13, most methods recovered some L6 and one method all T13; an iterative last-vertebra-anchored labeller
mislabelled the entire scan when an L6 was present [PMID 34340104]. Rib-side automation exists and is public: Moller et al.
2026, *AI* 7(6):224, doi:10.3390/ai7060224 (journal version of arXiv 2505.05004; masks and weights at the Zenodo concept
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14850928, whose single version is 14864106 — the folder notes cite the concept, `TRANSITIONAL_LABELS.csv`
the version; both are the same record) segment ribs at Dice 0.997 and separate stump from regular ribs at F1 0.84 from the
first ~22 mm of rib, with the stump label being the <= 38 mm rule on the automatic length, not a read; RibSeg v2 labels 24
ribs only and falls from 91.5 % to
79.6 % label accuracy on its challenging cases (missing floating ribs, possible 13th pair) [PMID 37695967]. TotalSegmentator
has no T13, L6, 13th-rib or lumbar-rib class (README, 117 classes; PMID 37795137 describes v1). Human reference: Castellvi
typing on CT by four readers against a fellowship-trained radiologist reaches sensitivity 76 %, specificity 93 %, kappa
