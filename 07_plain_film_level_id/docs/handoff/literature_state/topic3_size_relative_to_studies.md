# Topic 3. Size relative to other studies

Written 2026-10-08 from files on this laptop only. There was no network access and nothing was re-verified on the web. Each published study's n and result is copied from a prior report and is marked with that report's key; read every study cell as "as recorded in <key>". The programme's own numbers come from the manifest, the SchwingNet tables, the RSNA AI claims ledger and the memory notes, and each one carries its key. Ratios are programme n divided by study n, computed by `scratchpad/litstate/scripts/t3_ratios.py` and one inline check. They compare **size only**. The prior reports found that no two systems in this space report the same quantity ([CB]; [RF18] finding 15), so the headline results sit side by side and are not a ranking.

**Where the programme stands.** **Comparable** on CT size, with ratios of 0.52 to 2.7. It is **smaller** than VERIDAH's private cohort (0.52 times) and Schinz (0.65 times), and **below** TotalSegmentator, CTSpine1K and CTPelvic1K in scans (0.67 to 0.80 times). On transitional persons it is **comparable** to VERIDAH (129 against about 197 anomaly scans, inferred from rates). Most of its L6 set is VerSe's own (86 of 110), so beyond VerSe the programme adds 24 L6 persons.

**The programme's own prevalence-type numbers.** The 1,113 flagged CT spines are **not a prevalence sample**: VerSe is enriched by design (TLJ row 1), and CTSpinoPelvic1K is a colonography cohort aged 50 and over (MPDA). The tier-1 acquisition holds 15,233 CTs (people, at most) with an **expected** 831 to 1,703 transitional people. These are expected values from cited prevalences, not calls (ACQ).

**Where the programme stands.** **Smaller** than the large prevalence series: 0.13 to 0.35 times Paik, Tang, Nardo, Hanhivaara 2020 and Sabri. **Comparable** to Doo 2020 and Sugiura (0.83 and 0.94 times). **Larger** than Nagata, Yoon, Tins, Apazidis and Carrino (2.0 to 7.6 times). It is not designed to estimate prevalence. If the tier-1 screen finishes as planned, its screened CT count would be 1.84 times Paik's 8,280, but nothing on disk yet calls those people.
