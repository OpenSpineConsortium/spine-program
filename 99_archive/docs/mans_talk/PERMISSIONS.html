Figure permissions — Reading Spine MRI

23 figures are live on openspineconsortium.com/spine-mri/ marked permission pending, from 7 papers across 4 publishers. Four of the seven are the same publisher, so this is four requests.

All four publishers run permissions through RightsLink (Copyright Clearance Center). You will need one free CCC account — make it once on the first request and reuse it.

Submit and track everything here →

RightsLink · CCC Marketplace — marketplace.copyright.com

Make the free account on this page first (Sign in → Create account), then use the per-request links below. Every request you file lands under My Account → Orders on this same site, whichever publisher you came from — that is where grants, prices and refusals come back.

Best practice: start from the publisher’s own “Request Permissions” button (the click paths below) rather than searching inside Marketplace — arriving that way pre-fills the article, so you cannot license the wrong paper.

Fill the form the same way every time

RightsLink asks the same questions whichever publisher you came from. These are the answers for this project. Read the two marked ⚠ before you start.

Will it be sold / behind a paywall? No
URL where it will appear https://openspineconsortium.com/spine-mri/

⚠ Answer “yes” to modifying the content. Almost none of these are straight reproductions — multi-panel figures were cropped to single panels, and the Lee figure is an assembly of ten panels from six figures into one new image. Some publishers price or route a derivative differently. Declaring it costs nothing; being found to have under-declared it is the expensive version.

If a free text box appears, this is the description that helps most:

Free, openly accessible online educational series for advanced practice providers and trainees. Non-commercial, no paywall, no advertising. Full citation and a link to the original article appear in the caption of every figure.

Nothing needed for the other 23

The remaining figures are CC BY or CC BY-NC — attribution only, already printed in every caption. Origin: figures/from_web/SOURCES.md.

One to watch: the truncation-artefact “lip sign” figure in Part 1 is CC BY-NC. Fine while the series is free. If any of this ever sits behind paid CME or sponsorship, that image must come out.

If something is refused or priced

  • Bednarik, Kato, Nouri, Arvin — single supporting panels. Drop them; the surrounding text carries the argument.
  • Pfirrmann — Part 3 §2 is built on all four grades. Replacing them means shooting the equivalent from your own PACS. A real afternoon, but doable.
  • Schizas — the hard one. Part 2 depends on it end to end: the reference chart, the 66 mm² case, the overlap plot, the drill. There is no open-licence substitute for that grading figure. If refused, Part 2 becomes text plus your own cases.

Separately: asking the authors for originals

Not a permissions route — authors cannot grant it. But they can send high-resolution originals, which would genuinely improve the site: everything currently live was extracted from PDFs at publication resolution, and the Lee panels had to be re-rendered from the page because the embedded image streams came out inverted.

Say the word and I will draft those into your Outlook Drafts folder for you to review and send. Outlook is already set up for this on your machine — no credentials, no connector, nothing to authorise.
